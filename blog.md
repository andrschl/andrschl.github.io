Welcome to my (future) blog. Posts will appear here once added to `_posts/`.

To add your first post, create a file named `_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` with front matter like:

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "My first robotics note"
date: 2024-11-01
---

Content goes here.
```
